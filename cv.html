Research Profile

AI/ML researcher with 15+ years of academic and industry experience in machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, data mining, and multimodal learning. Current research focuses on AI-driven solutions for precision agriculture, environmental monitoring, healthcare, and other data-intensive applications, building on extensive experience solving real-world problems in academia and industry. Scholarly contributions include 20+ peer-reviewed journal and conference articles, a U.S. provisional patent, competitive research funding, and peer-review service for leading AI/ML conferences and journals.

Research interests: Generative AI · Deep Learning · NLP · Computer Vision · Precision Agriculture · Environmental Monitoring · Healthcare AI · Data Mining
